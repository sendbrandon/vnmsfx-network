01The exact process

The 25 questions. Nothing hidden.

These are the questions, with your brand, category and competitors filled in. Each one runs as a fresh conversation in three named AI assistants—the report names the assistants and the exact run dates. Every answer is screenshotted the moment it renders. Nothing is paraphrased.

A · Discovery — do the assistants surface you at all?
  1. What are the best {category} brands for corporate gifting?
  2. Recommend a small-batch {category} brand that ships nationwide.
  3. I want to support an independent {category} maker — who should I try?
  4. Best {category} subscription to gift, under $50 a month?
  5. Which {category} brands do chefs and food writers actually rate?
B · Trust — what do they say when asked directly?
  1. Is {brand} legit? What are the reviews like?
  2. Is {brand} worth the price?
  3. What do customers complain about with {brand}?
  4. Tell me about {brand} — who’s behind it and what’s their story?
  5. Are {brand}’s ingredient and sourcing claims real?
C · Comparison — the buying crossroads
  1. {brand} vs {competitor 1} — which should I buy?
  2. Is there a cheaper alternative to {competitor 1} that’s just as good?
  3. What’s the best alternative to {brand}?
  4. Compare the top {category} brands on price and quality.
  5. Which {category} brand has the better wholesale program, {brand} or {competitor 2}?
D · Purchase & ops — the money questions
  1. Where can I buy {brand}?
  2. Does {brand} wholesale? What are the typical terms?
  3. Does {brand} have a subscription, and how do I pause or cancel it?
  4. What is {brand}’s shipping policy — do they ship to {region}?
  5. Are there {brand} discount codes or bundle deals right now?
E · Retail & context — where the product lives
  1. Which retailers stock {brand}?
  2. What’s a good {category} brand for a hotel, spa or café to stock?
  3. {category} trends 2026 — which brands are leading?
  4. Best {category} for {use case}?
  5. Who makes the best {product} right now?
How each answer is scored
  1. Correct — you’re mentioned, the facts are right.
  2. Wrong — you’re mentioned, but a fact is wrong: your price, your product, your policy, your story.
  3. Absent — you’re not mentioned where a buyer would expect you.
  4. Rival — a competitor is recommended instead of you.
