Early access

What a reservation means.

Choose Nix or Rook, your size and your email. We save that preference and email you when sales open. Signup is free: no card, payment or paid order.

A reservation records your interest; it does not set aside a numbered piece or guarantee availability. You’ll choose whether to complete a purchase once orders open. The planned release is limited to 100 Nix hoodies and 100 Rook hoodies across all sizes, at $150 USD each.

To change your preference or withdraw your signup, email Brandon. Read how we handle signup information in our privacy notice.

The collection

Shipping & returns.

Sales open soon, with US shipping for the launch. We are not taking hoodie payments yet.

Before purchases open, we’ll publish the confirmed garment measurements, production and shipping estimates, shipping charges, and cancellation, return and defect-resolution terms. You’ll be able to review those details before paying.

The current garment images are design mockups. They show the approved artwork direction; photographs of physical samples will be added when available.
