BEFORE YOU BUY

WHAT THIS IS, AND WHAT IT ISN’T.

Stated here rather than discovered after payment.

A reference, not a cohort

This is a download: PDFs, plain-text prompts, images, templates and scripts. There are no live calls, no community and no scheduled sessions.

You bring the tools

Tool subscriptions and generation credits are separate and stay yours. The method survives a change of tool; the exact settings are recorded for the ones used.

The finish kit is technical

It runs in Terminal using Bash, FFmpeg and Python 3, with setup instructions included. Licensed CineGrain plates are not bundled; a procedural grain alternative is.

Results are reported, not promised

Client outcomes named inside are reported by Brandon Adams and describe those campaigns. Nothing here forecasts what your own work will do.
