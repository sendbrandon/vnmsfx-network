DATASecurity

How client data is handled.

Plain words, no diagram required. This page describes the standing policy for every engagement; each client's written agreement restates it.

The flow

Your systems stay the system of record. Data moves one way:

YOUR ACCOUNTS (Shopify · QuickBooks · HubSpot · Notion · inbox · distributor statements & settlement files)   ↓  read access, under your logins PROCESSING (business-tier AI services, commercial terms)   ↓  findings, drafts, reports BACK INTO YOUR ACCOUNTS — nothing acts without your approval

Outputs — audit findings, dispute drafts, dashboards — are written into accounts you own. There is no VNMSFX platform holding your data.

Access

  • Least-privilege: only the systems the engagement needs, read-only wherever the work allows.
  • Access is granted by you, under your accounts, and revocable by you in one click at any time.
  • Offboarding is a checklist: access revoked, working files removed, confirmed in writing. Everything built stays behind, working.

AI processing

  • Client data is processed only through business-tier AI services under commercial terms (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) — never through personal consumer chat accounts.
  • Under those commercial terms, submitted data is not used to train the providers' models.
  • A human approves before anything acts: no automated action touches your books, your customers, or your vendors without sign-off.

What I never need

Card numbers, bank credentials, Social Security numbers, or employee personal files. If a system holds them, access is scoped so they stay out of view.

Agreements & incidents

  • A mutual NDA and written data-handling terms are available before any access is granted — just ask.
  • If anything ever goes wrong with your data, you hear about it from me first, in plain words, within 72 hours.
