DATASecurity

Before VNMSFX handles client data.

Do not send passwords or confidential records through this website or the free teardown. Private-data work does not begin until the exact access, AI tools, workspace, data rules and end-of-project steps are written, approved and tested.

The short version

YOUR EXISTING TOOLS AND FILES   ↓  the agreed checks run RESULTS GO TO YOUR WORKSPACE   ↓  your named person reviews YOUR TEAM DECIDES AND ACTS

The standard system does not change your books, orders, inventory or payments, and it does not send customer or vendor messages. It shows the agreed records, what needs attention and where the answer came from. Your team makes the decision.

Before paid work begins

  • The written agreement names every tool, record, date range and field the project may use.
  • It names how access is provided, what the access can actually do and who can remove it.
  • It names the exact AI product and account, what it may receive and how long it may keep data.
  • It names the folder or workspace your company owns where results will live.
  • It names how working copies are handled, when they are deleted and how access is removed when work ends.

How access works

Access must use an account in my name that you can remove, a separate account your company creates for the system, a permission screen you approve, or an export you provide. VNMSFX never asks for a shared employee password.

If a tool cannot limit the connection to viewing only, I tell you before connecting. You approve the wider permission or provide recurring exports instead.

Where records and results live

Your original records stay in your existing tools. Project results go only to the restricted folder or workspace your company controls.

The signed agreement must state whether any temporary working copy is needed, where it lives and its deletion date. No private record is accepted until that route has been tested.

How AI is used

AI may help read, sort, summarize and draft. It does not get to decide whether a leak exists.

An important answer must show the original records, the agreed rule and what is missing. Important math is checked outside AI. If the records do not support the answer, it is marked for review instead of presented as fact.

Your finance person owns accounting rules and important financial decisions. VNMSFX organizes the evidence and runs the approved check.

What I never need

Card numbers, bank credentials, Social Security numbers, or employee personal files. If a system holds them, access is scoped so they stay out of view.

When work ends

There is no universal one-click removal. Each account and connection must be removed in the tool that created it. The final handover includes a written list of what was transferred, removed or deleted and anything still waiting on an outside provider.

The signed agreement must say who to contact if something goes wrong, who must be told and how every account and connection will be removed when work ends.

Current boundary: the website and free teardown collect no passwords, private exports, accounting rows, customer lists or confidential screen-share. Paid client-data work opens only after these controls have been configured and tested for the engagement.
