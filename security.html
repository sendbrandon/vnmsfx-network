DATASecurity

How client data is handled.

Plain words, no diagram required. This page describes the standing policy for every engagement; each client's written agreement restates it.

The flow

Your systems stay the system of record. Data moves one way:

YOUR ACCOUNTS (Shopify · QuickBooks · HubSpot · Notion · inbox · distributor statements & settlement files)   ↓  read access via named collaborator, service account, OAuth or export PROCESSING (named business-tier AI processors, commercial terms, no training on your data)   ↓  findings, drafts, reports APPROVED OUTPUTS WRITTEN TO NAMED DESTINATIONS — agreed in writing before work starts; nothing acts without your approval

Outputs — audit findings, dispute drafts, dashboards — are written into accounts you own. There is no VNMSFX platform holding your data.

Access

  • Least-privilege: only the systems the engagement needs, read-only wherever the work allows.
  • Access is provided inside client-owned accounts through a named collaborator account, a dedicated service account, OAuth, or a client-created export. VNMSFX never asks for or uses a shared employee password.
  • Each grant is revocable in the system that issued it. Offboarding follows and verifies a provider-by-provider checklist — not a single switch — and ends in a written receipt listing what was removed.
  • Before work begins we document, in writing, what can be read, where approved outputs may be written, which processors are involved, and how every grant is removed at offboarding. What was built stays in your accounts.

AI processing

  • Client data is processed only through business-tier AI products under commercial terms — never personal consumer chat accounts. The exact products, account owner, processing mode, training setting and retention behaviour are named in your engagement data schedule before access is granted.
  • Under those commercial terms, submitted data is not used to train the providers' models.
  • A human approves before anything acts: no automated action touches your books, your customers, or your vendors without sign-off.

What I never need

Card numbers, bank credentials, Social Security numbers, or employee personal files. If a system holds them, access is scoped so they stay out of view.

Agreements & incidents

  • A mutual NDA and written data-handling terms are available before any access is granted — just ask.
  • If anything ever goes wrong with your data, you hear about it from me first, in plain words. The written notification timetable is set in your agreement.
