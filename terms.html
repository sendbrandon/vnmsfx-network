Terms, in plain words.

The standing terms for VNMSFX engagements. Every engagement also gets a short written agreement; where they differ, the signed agreement governs.

The offers

  • Teardown — a free 30-minute working session. No obligation either way.
  • Leak Audit — $2,500. One agreed operating area and three written questions, delivered within five business days of Audit Ready. The Audit is a snapshot review with no implementation. It includes evidence-backed answers, a Build Map and a 60-minute walkthrough.
  • Standard Build. The complete Audit-to-handover engagement totals $15,000. The Build delivers three tested Working Checks using up to four agreed sources across the full engagement. The first check is live within 10 business days of Build Ready, all three are live by the end of week eight, and month three covers reliability, training and handover. Thirty days of defect correction follow final acceptance.

A paid, non-refunded Audit credits into Build month one when the client signs the Build agreement and pays the first Build installment within 60 calendar days of Audit delivery. The payment order is $2,500 for the Audit, $2,500 for Build month one after the Audit credit, $5,000 for month two and $5,000 for month three.

The standard Build is offered only when the Audit identifies three build-ready checks. If it identifies only one or two, the client keeps the completed Audit and VNMSFX does not sell the standard Build. A smaller or custom Build requires a separate written offer and price.

When the clocks start

Audit Ready Date: the written start date after the Audit agreement and data schedule are signed, the $2,500 payment has cleared, all agreed records, access and client decisions needed for the three questions are ready, and VNMSFX has passed the two-business-day readiness check. Booking a call or submitting a request does not start paid work.

Build Ready Date: the objective date when the Build agreement and data schedule are signed, the first Build payment has cleared, all included access and sample records are available, and the named client decisions and approvers are complete. Source feasibility, fallbacks, acceptance examples and rules are locked in the signed agreement. VNMSFX records the date in writing within one business day; recording it cannot postpone or create the date.

What if the Audit finds nothing?

VNMSFX will not invent a leak to justify a Build. Before payment, the Audit schedule names the three questions, review period, sources, rules, thresholds, exclusions and the standard-Build check each question could support.

If the completed Audit tests all three questions and documents zero qualifying operating issues under those written rules, VNMSFX refunds the full $2,500 through the original payment route within five business days. The client keeps the completed report. A refunded Audit cannot also be used as a Build credit.

  • If VNMSFX approves the supplied data and then discovers before substantive analysis that it cannot test a question, the client chooses a written rescope or a full refund.
  • The client has 10 business days after secure intake opens to provide the signed-scope records, access and decisions. After a written five-business-day cure notice, incomplete intake ends the Audit with a full refund. No Audit is claimed.
  • If the client removes or materially changes required access after Audit Ready, the clock pauses and a written five-business-day cure notice is issued. If access is not restored, the Audit ends without a refund and VNMSFX delivers the evidence-backed work completed through the interruption.
  • Missing records never become a finding.

What counts as delivered

Done means tested—not demonstrated. A Working Check is delivered when it passes the agreed examples, shows the proof behind every important result, flags records it cannot trust, reproduces important arithmetic outside AI and can be run by the client's trained operator without VNMSFX. Standard scope presents the proposed next step to a named human; it does not execute the external action.

VNMSFX includes 30 calendar days of defect support after final acceptance. A covered defect is a reproducible failure of the accepted system to meet its written test under the accepted setup. A covered defect reported during the 30-day window remains covered until it is corrected and retested.

New sources, rules, outputs, client changes and removed access are not original defects. A third-party outage or provider change is not itself an original defect when it falls outside the accepted fault-handling boundary. If an accepted Working Check was required to detect, stop or visibly route that event and fails to do so, that failure remains covered.

If an approved source cannot work

VNMSFX does not silently remove another check, stretch the schedule or raise the price. The client chooses a tested recurring-file fallback, a comparable source that answers the same question with equivalent evidence, or a written change to scope, timing and price.

If VNMSFX approved a source as feasible and no equivalent standard-scope route exists, the affected undeliverable allocation is credited against unpaid fees or refunded through the original payment route.

Costs outside the $15,000

Approved software, AI, API, automation and hosting costs are paid directly by the client and are separate from the $15,000. Before Build Ready, the signed scope lists the exact products, existing versus new costs, expected monthly range, usage assumptions and spending limits.

What you own

Everything built for you — connections, prompts, documents, dashboards — is yours on payment, living in accounts you control. Excluded: third-party software subscriptions (which are yours directly) and pre-existing methods VNMSFX brings to every engagement.

What is promised — and what is not

Findings are documented with their sources so you can verify every number. What is not promised: a specific dollar amount of recovered money. Anyone promising that before seeing your books is guessing.

VNMSFX provides decision support, not accounting, legal, or investment advice; your accountant and counsel stay your accountant and counsel.

The boring but important part

  • Confidentiality is mutual and survives the engagement. The agreement, confidentiality terms and data schedule are completed automatically before VNMSFX accepts credentials, private exports, accounting rows, customer data or confidential live screen-share.
  • Liability under any engagement is capped at the fees actually paid for it.
