The offer, in plain words.

This page summarizes the business offer. It is not a client agreement. No payment or private records are collected through this website. Every project gets a written scope and agreement; the signed agreement controls.

The offers

  • Teardown — a free 30-minute working session. No obligation either way.
  • Leak Audit — $2,500. One agreed area and three written questions. Once the agreement, payment, records and access are ready, VNMSFX delivers the Audit within five business days. You receive three answers backed by the records, a Build Map and a 60-minute walkthrough. The Audit does not install or run anything.
  • Standard Build — $5,000 a month for three months. The Build creates three running checks using up to four tools or data sources across the full project. The first check runs within 10 business days after the Build agreement, first payment, access and client decisions are ready. All three run by the end of week eight. Month three covers reliability, training and handover.

A paid, non-refunded Audit credits into Build month one only when the client signs the Build agreement and pays the first Build installment within 60 calendar days of Audit delivery. The payment order is $2,500 for the Audit, $2,500 for Build month one after the Audit credit, $5,000 for month two and $5,000 for month three.

After 60 days, the credit ends and the old Audit can no longer start the Build. A new Audit at the current price is required. At today’s prices, starting again would be a new $15,000 Audit-to-handover project; the earlier $2,500 would remain money already spent. A refunded Audit cannot also receive a Build credit.

The standard Build is offered only when the Audit identifies three useful checks that can honestly be built and tested. If it identifies only one or two, the client keeps the completed Audit and VNMSFX does not sell the standard Build. No smaller or custom first Build is currently offered.

When the work starts

The Audit clock starts only after the Audit agreement and data rules are signed, payment has cleared, the agreed records, access and client decisions are ready, and VNMSFX confirms the start in writing. Booking a call or asking to start does not begin paid work. If everything required sits ready for two business days without a written start or explanation from VNMSFX, the client may cancel for a full refund until that decision arrives.

The Build clock starts when the Build agreement and data rules are signed, the first Build payment has cleared, the included access and sample records are available, and the named client decisions are complete. The exact sources, fallbacks, examples and rules are written before that clock begins.

What if the Audit finds nothing?

VNMSFX will not invent a leak to justify a Build. Before payment, the written scope names the three questions, records, dates and exact rule for each.

If all three tests are completed and the records support no issue under those rules, VNMSFX refunds the full $2,500 to the original payment method within five business days. The client keeps the completed report, and VNMSFX does not recommend a standard Build from that Audit.

A real issue must be inside the agreed question and dates, be backed by the named records, meet the rule set before payment and appear in the completed report. A hunch, missing file, possible upside or unverified lead does not count. Missing records alone never become a leak.

  • If VNMSFX approved the scope and later discovers that one of the three questions cannot be tested, work pauses. The client chooses a revised written plan with new dates or a full refund and receives the work completed to that point.
  • The client has 10 business days after the secure record-sharing route opens to provide the agreed records, access and decisions. After a written reminder and five more business days, an incomplete setup ends with a full refund because the Audit never started.
  • If the client removes required access after the Audit starts, the clock pauses. VNMSFX gives written notice and five business days to restore it. If access is not restored, the Audit ends without a refund and the client receives the work completed before the interruption. If VNMSFX caused the access failure, the revised-plan-or-refund rule above applies.
  • Missing records never become a finding.

What counts as delivered

Done means tested—not demonstrated. A running check is delivered when it passes the agreed examples, shows the proof behind every important result, puts records it cannot trust on a review list, checks important math outside AI and can be run by the client’s trained person without VNMSFX. The system shows the next step; the client takes the action.

Handover is complete only after all three checks, the shared workspace, the instructions and the training pass the agreed tests. Starting the next day, VNMSFX includes 30 days of no-cost fixes when the accepted system fails one of those written tests. A failure reported during the 30 days stays covered until it is corrected and retested.

New sources, new rules, new outputs, client changes and removed access are new work. An outside tool changing or going down is not automatically a VNMSFX failure. If the agreed check was supposed to catch or flag that problem and failed to do so, VNMSFX fixes that failure.

If an approved source cannot work

VNMSFX does not silently remove another check, stretch the schedule or raise the price. One store account, company file, mailbox or regularly delivered file uses one of the four available source spots once work begins, even if it later fails.

If a connection fails, the client may use a tested export from the same account, use another source while one of the four spots remains, or approve a written change. A fifth source is outside this offer.

If VNMSFX approved a source and then cannot deliver the affected part of the Build, the signed agreement explains which unpaid fee is removed or which paid fee is returned. The exact fee split must be agreed before work starts.

Costs outside the $15,000

Third-party tools are paid directly by the client with no VNMSFX markup and are separate from the $15,000. Most small-brand Builds should plan for about $100–$300 a month. A lean setup may be $50–$125; a complex or high-volume setup may be $300–$800 or more.

Before the Build agreement is signed or its first payment is made, the client receives the exact required and optional tools, what is already being paid, any new monthly or annual cost, what changes the bill and the spending limit the client approved. The Audit normally requires no new recurring production tool; any exception is disclosed before the Audit agreement or payment.

What you own

Your data and accounts remain yours. After payment, you own the client-specific results and materials listed in the signed agreement. Third-party software and VNMSFX methods used across projects are not transferred.

What is promised — and what is not

Important results are linked to the records behind them. What is not promised: a specific amount of recovered money. The Audit can show a missed item, a disputable amount or something that needs attention without pretending the money will be recovered.

VNMSFX provides decision support, not accounting, legal, or investment advice; your accountant and counsel stay your accountant and counsel.

The written agreement

The final signed agreement covers confidentiality, data handling, ownership, payment, cancellation, limits and governing law. Those legal terms require review in the client agreement; this website summary does not replace them.

VNMSFX accepts no passwords, private exports, accounting rows, customer data or confidential screen-share before the required written terms are complete and the approved way to receive records has been tested.
