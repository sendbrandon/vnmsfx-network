01Wholesale

Catch problems after the account or order has already been won.

A distributor deducts money and the proof is scattered

A distributor pays you less and says cases were missing. The purchase order, warehouse record and delivery proof are sitting in different places while the deadline to challenge it gets closer.

WHAT VNMSFX BUILDS

A system that flags the deadline, gathers the matching records and prepares one evidence package for your team to review.

A good wholesale account stops ordering

A retailer normally orders every 30 to 45 days. Day 80 arrives, but nobody notices and no follow-up happens.

WHAT VNMSFX BUILDS

A system that flags accounts that have gone quiet and shows the last order, previous order history, account owner and suggested next step.

02Direct to Consumer

Catch problems between the campaign, the offer and the store.

An ad keeps sending customers somewhere they cannot buy

The product is sold out. The page is broken. The ad points to the wrong item. Or the offer in the ad has expired.

WHAT VNMSFX BUILDS

A system that checks active campaigns against product pages, inventory and offer dates, then flags anything that does not match.

A discount keeps running longer than intended

An influencer code was supposed to end months ago. Or customers are combining discounts that were never meant to work together.

WHAT VNMSFX BUILDS

A system that checks discount use against your approved dates and rules and shows the affected orders and discount amounts.

03Subscriptions

Catch failed payments and broken handoffs before customers disappear.

A payment fails and the follow-up never happens

A customer’s card expires or a recurring payment fails. The retry or message does not happen, and the subscription eventually disappears.

WHAT VNMSFX BUILDS

A system that flags failed payments that still need attention and shows the customer history, retry status and next step.

The customer paid, but the order or shipment is missing

The payment succeeded, but no order was created. Or the order exists, but the warehouse never received it.

WHAT VNMSFX BUILDS

A system that checks for a matching payment, order and shipment—and flags the missing step.
