01Selling direct

AAds pointing at what you can’t ship

What gets missed

An ad keeps spending after the product sells out, the page breaks or the promotion expires. The ad platform, store and inventory each show part of the problem—but nobody is checking them together.

What it costs you

You keep paying for visits that cannot become the intended purchase, and customers who were ready to buy reach a dead end.

What VNMSFX builds

VNMSFX builds a system that compares active campaigns with product pages, inventory and offer dates. It alerts the right person while the ad is still spending so the team can pause, redirect or correct it.

BDiscounts that give away more than you planned

What gets missed

A weekend influencer code is still active six months later. Or codes, automatic discounts, subscription pricing and free shipping combine in ways nobody approved. Each rule lives in a different place, so nobody sees the full discount until after the order.

What it costs you

You give away more than you planned on every affected order.

What VNMSFX builds

VNMSFX builds a system that compares the discounts used with the dates and rules you approved. It shows the affected orders and amounts, then sends anything outside the rule to the person responsible.

02Wholesale

ADistributor deductions and shortages

What gets missed

A distributor pays less and says cases were missing. The deduction is in a PDF, the purchase order is in email and the warehouse record is in the 3PL portal. Nobody brings them together before the dispute deadline expires.

What it costs you

The brand accepts a smaller payment or loses the chance to challenge it—not because the proof did not exist, but because it stayed scattered too long.

What VNMSFX builds

VNMSFX builds a system that checks each deduction against its deadline and gathers the matching purchase order, warehouse record and delivery proof into one package. Your team decides whether to challenge it.

BOrders that stall before they ship

What gets missed

A retailer sends a purchase order. Sales thinks it is accepted, but inventory is short, the warehouse never receives the instruction or the ship date passes without anyone telling the retailer.

What it costs you

The retailer may cancel, reduce future orders or issue a shortage deduction. The order was already won; the work between sale and shipment broke down.

What VNMSFX builds

VNMSFX builds a system that checks accepted orders against inventory, warehouse activity, promised ship dates and delivered quantities. It shows the right person what is late, short or missing while there is still time to act.

03Subscriptions

AFailed payments that never recover

What gets missed

A subscriber’s card expires and the charge fails. The retry never succeeds, the reminder does not go out or nobody sees which accounts still need attention. The subscription eventually cancels.

What it costs you

The customer may not have chosen to leave. The payment failed, and the follow-up stopped before the problem was resolved.

What VNMSFX builds

VNMSFX builds a system that shows the failed charge, subscriber history, retry status, message status and next approved step. Your team handles the customer.

04Buyer and account follow-up

AAccounts that go quiet

What gets missed

A retailer normally orders every 30 to 45 days. It has now been 80. The last order is in one tool, the account owner is in another and nobody is comparing the two.

What it costs you

A proven account can cool off without anyone checking in. You spend time and money winning the relationship, then learn about the silence only after weeks or months have passed.

What VNMSFX builds

VNMSFX builds a system that checks each account against its own ordering pattern. When one goes quiet, it shows the last order, prior history, account owner and a drafted check-in.

BFollow-ups that never went out

What gets missed

A buyer asks for samples at a trade show. A quote goes out with no reminder. A broker introduction sits in an inbox and never enters a shared pipeline.

What it costs you

You spend on trade shows, samples, brokers and travel—and real buyer opportunities disappear because nobody owns the next action.

What VNMSFX builds

VNMSFX builds a system that finds buyer inquiries, samples, quotes and introductions with no owner or an overdue next step. It puts them in one list with the last activity and the person responsible.
