01Sales

Before the account is won, keep real buyer conversations moving.

Your sales pipeline lives in inboxes and memory.

One person has the hotel contact. Another has the distributor email. Retailer notes are in a spreadsheet. Nobody can see the full picture or who owns the next move.

WHY IT HURTS

A real sales opportunity does not have to reject you. It can disappear because nobody followed up.

WHAT VNMSFX BUILDS

A simple sales system that keeps the company, contact, sales stage, owner, last conversation and next step in one place—and flags conversations that are going quiet.

The samples arrived. The deal stopped moving.

The buyer asked for samples. You sent them. They arrived. Then nobody recorded when or how to follow up.

WHY IT HURTS

You earned the buyer’s interest and paid to support the conversation. Then the opportunity was left to cool in somebody’s inbox.

WHAT VNMSFX BUILDS

A system that shows which quotes, samples, meetings and contract conversations have stalled, what happened last and who owns the next step.

02Wholesale

After the account is won, protect the order and the relationship.

Your distributor pays you less. Your proof is scattered.

The purchase order is in email. The warehouse record is in the 3PL portal. The delivery proof is somewhere else. Meanwhile, the deadline to challenge the deduction keeps getting closer.

WHY IT HURTS

The deadline can pass while the evidence sits untouched in different systems.

WHAT VNMSFX BUILDS

A system that flags the deadline, brings the matching records together and prepares one evidence package for your team to review.

A good wholesale account goes quiet. Nobody notices.

The retailer normally orders every 30 to 45 days. Now it has been 80. The CRM still says active, but nobody owns the follow-up.

WHY IT HURTS

You did the hard work of winning the account. Then the relationship can fade while your team is busy chasing new business.

WHAT VNMSFX BUILDS

A system that flags accounts that have gone quiet and shows the last order, previous order history, account owner and suggested next step.

03Direct to Consumer

You paid for the attention. Make sure customers can buy what they clicked.

You are paying to send shoppers somewhere they cannot buy.

The product is sold out. The page is broken. The ad points to the wrong item. Or the offer shown in the ad has expired.

WHY IT HURTS

You keep paying for visits that cannot complete the purchase shown in the ad.

WHAT VNMSFX BUILDS

A system that checks active campaigns against product pages, inventory and offer dates, then flags anything that does not match.

The promotion ended. The discount did not.

An influencer code was supposed to stop months ago. Or customers are combining discounts that were never intended to work together.

WHY IT HURTS

Customers keep receiving a deal you never meant to keep giving.

WHAT VNMSFX BUILDS

A system that checks discount use against your approved dates and rules and shows the affected orders and discount amounts.

04Subscriptions

A broken payment or handoff should not quietly cost you a customer.

Their payment fails. Your follow-up fails too.

A customer’s card expires or a recurring payment fails. The retry or message does not happen, and the subscription eventually disappears.

WHY IT HURTS

The customer may not have chosen to cancel. The recovery process simply stopped before anybody finished it.

WHAT VNMSFX BUILDS

A system that flags failed payments that still need attention and shows the customer history, retry status and next step.

They paid you. The order or shipment is missing.

The payment succeeded, but no order was created. Or the order exists, but the warehouse never received it.

WHY IT HURTS

The customer has paid, but the product may never leave the warehouse. Your team may not discover the problem until the customer complains.

WHAT VNMSFX BUILDS

A system that checks for a matching payment, order and shipment—and flags the missing step.
