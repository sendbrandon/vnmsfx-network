Before you join the trouble.

Is the club free?+

Yes. Applying and participating are free. The club is separate from paid episodes and viewing memberships; it doesn’t include paid episode access.

Do I need a big following?+

No. A thoughtful introduction to a few friends matters. We’re interested in your connection to the film and how you’d enjoy taking part.

What am I committing to?+

Applying doesn’t commit you to a posting schedule or a quota. If selected, you’ll receive an invitation with the next activity, and you can decide whether to take part. You can leave at any time.

What happens after I apply?+

Your application goes to VNMSFX for review. We’ll email selected founding members with next steps. Invitations, activities and release dates will be announced when they’re ready.
