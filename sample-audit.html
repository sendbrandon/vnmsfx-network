SAMPLE AUDIT · FICTIONAL EXAMPLE

You already won the customer, the order and the buyer’s attention. Then the follow-through broke.

This fictional food and beverage brand sells wholesale, direct to consumers, through subscriptions and to hotels and retailers. The products are not the problem. Important deadlines, handoffs and follow-ups are being missed behind the scenes. Here is what the VNMSFX Audit surfaced—and the systems worth building first.

This company and its records are invented. A real Audit looks at one area at a time and only at the records agreed with the client.

Four problems hiding in plain sight.

Sales

Wholesale

Direct to Consumer

Subscriptions

Customer paid. Shipment record missing.

FINDING 01Sales

You sent the samples. Nobody followed up.

What the records show

A hotel buyer asked for pricing and samples. The samples arrived 19 days ago. No owner, follow-up date or next step was recorded.

Why it hurts

You did the hard part: earned a real buyer’s interest. Then the relationship was left to cool in somebody’s inbox.

What VNMSFX would build

A simple sales system that keeps the company, contact, sales stage, owner, last conversation and next step in one place—and flags active conversations that are going quiet.

FINDING 02Wholesale

A good wholesale account went quiet. Nobody noticed.

What the records show

The retailer normally ordered every 30 to 45 days. It has now been 80 days. The account still says active, but no follow-up task is visible.

Why it hurts

Your team already did the hard work of winning the account. The relationship can fade while everybody is focused on finding new customers.

What VNMSFX would build

A system that flags accounts that have gone past the reorder timing your team chooses and shows the last order, account owner, last contact and suggested next step.

FINDING 03Direct to Consumer

Your ad is still running. The product is sold out.

What the records show

The campaign is active. The destination product has been unavailable for four days.

Why it hurts

Every new click lands on a dead end. You keep paying to disappoint someone who was ready to shop.

What VNMSFX would build

A system that checks active campaigns against product pages, inventory and offer dates, then flags anything that no longer matches.

FINDING 04Subscriptions

The customer paid. The order never appeared.

What the records show

The payment record shows a successful subscription renewal. The store and warehouse records do not show the matching order or shipment step.

Why it hurts

A paying customer may be waiting for an order your team believes is already handled.

What VNMSFX would build

A system that checks the expected path—payment, order and shipment—and flags the missing step for your team to review.
