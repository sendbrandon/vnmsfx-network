F-01Deductions worth disputing — $8,400/yr

READ: 14 settlement statements, line by line, both distributors, trailing 12 months

14 deduction lines met the distributors' own published dispute criteria — mostly short-ship claims on purchase orders the warehouse scans show picked and delivered in full. All 14 sit inside their 180-day dispute windows.

RECEIPT  settlement 2026-03-14 · line 27 · "shortage — 12 cs" · PO-4471 CROSS    BOL 88213 signed clean · WMS pick log 288/288 units WINDOW   filed within 180 days · distributor's own criteria met

DELIVERED: the 14-line dispute tracker, claim letters drafted per distributor format, filing order by deadline.

F-02Hero SKU losing $0.42 a unit wholesale — $19,700/yr

READ: landed cost build-up per SKU, freight allocated per case, promo accruals, deduction history — not just COGS

True contribution margin by SKU and channel. The best seller is comfortably profitable DTC and underwater at wholesale once freight and trade spend land on it — at 46,900 wholesale units a year, that's the biggest single number in the audit, moving in silence.

SKU-01 · WHOLESALE  price 2.10 − landed 1.64 − freight .31 − trade .57 = −0.42 SKU-01 · DTC        price 3.99 − landed 1.64 − ship 1.02 − fees .48 = +0.85 VOLUME  46,900 units/yr wholesale → −$19,698 annualized

DELIVERED: the margin sheet for all six SKUs × three channels, every input tied to its source line — plus the two price/pack moves that flip it positive, modeled.

YOUR CALL price move vs. pack-size move vs. exit the account tier — the decision log holds the trade-offs, one page.

F-03Two quiet accounts — $21,000/yr at risk

READ: reorder intervals for every active wholesale account, 24 months

Account #3 stretched from a 31-day to a 58-day cycle over five months; account #11 went from monthly to "when we remember." Invisible in monthly revenue — the totals were propped up by two new accounts — obvious in the order log. Nothing was listening.

ACCT-03  orders: Jan 4 · Feb 4 · Mar 8 · Apr 22 · Jun 19 INTERVAL 31d → 33d → 45d → 58d  ·  run-rate −$21k annualized combined

DELIVERED: the quiet-account watchlist ranked by run-rate at risk, outreach drafted per account, and the alert that fires at +20% interval stretch from now on.

F-042024 promo codes, still live — $6,300/yr

READ: all 9 active Shopify discount codes against their campaign end dates

Two codes from a fall 2024 campaign never got turned off. One stacks with free shipping. They've been circulating on coupon sites, clipping full-price orders that would have converted anyway.

FALL24   created 2024-09-12 · campaign ended 2024-11-30 · still active          412 uses since · 71% arrived via coupon aggregators

DELIVERED: the kill list, the two codes worth keeping, and the expiry-date rule so this never recurs.

F-05Parcel where LTL was cheaper — $4,900/yr

READ: 36 freight invoices against the carrier's own break-point table

88 outbound shipments crossed the weight where less-than-truckload beats parcel, and shipped parcel anyway — an average $56 overpay each. The picking software has the threshold; nobody had set it.

2026-04-08  6 cs / 214 lb → parcel $187 · LTL quote $121 · Δ $66 PATTERN    88 of 312 shipments over 150 lb went parcel · avg Δ $56

DELIVERED: the threshold rule, written for your 3PL. VERIFY WITH CARRIER — the LTL quotes are rate-card based; confirmed pricing may move the figure either way. The ledger keeps the flag until it's confirmed.

Three SKU-channel pairs run slower than their shelf life. The write-offs land as "surprises" every quarter, but the collision is visible 60 days out every time — long enough to move product to the fast channel or run a targeted clearance instead of a dumpster.

SKU-04 × RETAIL-EAST  velocity 310 u/mo · on hand 1,840 · BBD in 74 days MATH                    sell-through by BBD ≈ 765 → ~1,075 units at risk

DELIVERED: the watchlist that flags any lot 60 days before it strands, with the two standing plays (channel shift / targeted clearance) pre-written.

What lands in your accounts

The audit isn't a PDF that dies in a drive. It's delivered as a working system inside accounts you own — the same discipline every build gets: official values, statuses, receipts, follow-up queues.

  • The leak ledger — every finding with its official value, status, and source link. One number per finding; discrepancies get flagged, never averaged away.
  • The dispute tracker — one row per claim: amount, evidence, deadline, status from DRAFTED to PAID.
  • The margin workbook — six SKUs × three channels, every input traced to its source line, the two price/pack scenarios modeled.
  • The quiet-account watchlist — ranked by run-rate at risk, with the interval alert wired in.
  • The drafts folder — claim letters, recovery emails, account outreach: written, waiting on your approval. Nothing sends itself.
  • The decision log — the calls only you can make, one page each, trade-offs stated.
  • The walkthrough — 60 minutes, finding by finding. Then it's yours either way — and the $2,500 credits into the build if you continue.
