The 5-Minute Leak Check.

Twelve yes-or-no questions that show where a food and beverage business quietly loses money. Answer honestly—“not sure” is the most useful answer you can give.

No logins, no files, no email required. Your answers stay on this page: nothing is sent or stored when you score it.

0 / 12 answered ~5 min

01Can you trust your numbers?

Has anyone test-fired your website’s purchase tracking in the last 90 days?

Not “is it installed”—has someone run a test order and watched the sale get counted?

Do your ad platform and your store agree on last month’s sales?

Same date range, same definition of “a sale.” If you have never compared them, that is a “not sure.”

Are refunds subtracted before you judge whether a campaign worked?

The platform’s number and the money that actually stayed are not the same number.

02Money you’re owed

When a distributor pays less than invoiced, do you catch and challenge it before the deadline?

Deductions, chargebacks, “missing cases”—every one has a clock on it.

Would you know this week if a good wholesale account stopped ordering?

Without anyone having to remember to go look.

Do lapsed subscribers or repeat customers get noticed while they’re still warm?

Noticed by a system or a routine—not by luck.

03Follow-through

Does every promised follow-up—vendor, buyer, partner—have an owner and a due date written down?

Somewhere that gets checked. An inbox is not that place.

Could you answer “where does X stand right now?” in under a minute?

From a system—not by asking around.

If your busiest person went quiet for a week, would the team still know what’s owed and when?

Or does part of the business live only in their head?

04Handoffs

When a sale is won, is there a check that the warehouse actually got the instruction and shipped on time?

A check that runs—not an assumption.

Does information move between your tools without someone retyping it?

Orders into spreadsheets, emails into trackers, totals into reports.

When something goes wrong, can you trace what happened—order, email, file—in one place?

Or does someone have to rebuild the story by hand?
