WHAT YOUR TEAM LEARNS

LESS GUESSWORK.
MORE CONTROL.

We choose the parts your team needs. Every exercise has a clear job and something specific to review.

01 / THE IDEA

Give people a reason to watch.

Build an opening, a reason to keep watching and an ending that connects to what you sell.

Work on: a short ad direction.
02 / THE PRODUCT

Keep your product looking right.

Use source photos to control the shape, packaging, color and label. Learn to spot a result you can’t use.

Work on: product references and image direction.
03 / THE CAST

Create a character. Keep that character.

Choose a face, wardrobe and presence with a point of view. Keep the same person across the shots.

Work on: a character reference sheet.
04 / THE IMAGE

Choose the look before you generate.

Set the framing, light, texture and mood. Learn how a reference becomes a useful instruction.

Work on: a visual direction and working prompts.
05 / THE VIDEO

Make the shots tell one story.

Turn stills into scenes. Direct movement, preserve continuity and give each cut a reason to happen.

Work on: a shot sequence or short video exercise.
06 / THE FINISH

Know what to keep. Know what to fix.

Review product accuracy, faces, pacing and sound. Catch the details that make a good idea feel unfinished.

Work on: an edit review and quality checklist.

BEFORE YOU BOOK

A FEW
STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

Who is this for?

Brand marketing teams, in-house creatives and agencies that want more control over AI images and video. Tell me what the team already knows so we can set the right starting point.

What does it cost, and how long is it?

Both are quoted for your team and the work we agree to cover. Send the team size, goal and preferred timing. You’ll receive a proposed scope and fee before booking.

Which AI tools will we use?

We choose the tools around the exercises and what your team is allowed to use. Share any restrictions before booking. Required accounts, subscriptions or generation credits are identified in the quote.

Will we leave with a finished ad?

The workshop is for learning and guided practice. What we complete depends on the agreed scope, your team’s experience and generation time. If you need a finished commercial produced for your brand, see the commercial service.

Can we use our own product and brand assets?

Yes. We agree what to prepare before the session. Use assets your team is permitted to share and confirm which product details and claims must stay accurate.

Can we record the session?

Include that request in your inquiry. Any recording, internal reuse and related fee are agreed in advance.
