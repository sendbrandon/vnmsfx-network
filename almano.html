THE REVIEW

THE COUNTY WANTS
THE POWER OFF.

On day thirty, a judge decides. Until then, the Steward keeps the lights on. Follow the review, one day at a time.

In 1998, a company in the high desert sold forty people the promise of waking up in a better future. It went bankrupt in 2006. Everyone left except one employee, who still wears the uniform and still gives the tour.

Now the county has thirty days to decide whether to turn off the power. A judge who has no law for this. An insurance adjuster who has to put a price on a frozen person. A mayor who sold the policies in 1998 and has been billing the town for the electricity ever since. And the Steward, whose mother is in capsule nineteen.

New days post as the review continues. Enroll to be notified.
