Privacy, in plain words.

What this website collects, what it doesn't, and where the rest lives.

This website

  • vnmsfx.com sets no advertising trackers and runs no third-party ad pixels.
  • Hosting (Vercel) keeps standard server logs — IP address, browser, pages requested — for security and uptime.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives the standard request data involved in serving a file.
  • Page views and visits are counted with Vercel Web Analytics, served from this domain. It is aggregate and cookieless: no advertising identifier, no profile, and no following you to other websites.
  • If you arrive from a link that carries a campaign tag (utm_source and the like), the tag is held in your browser for that tab only and added to the booking or checkout link if you click one — so I can tell which message brought you. No cookies, no profile built, and nothing about you is sent anywhere by the page itself. Close the tab and it's gone.

Booking and payment

  • Calls are booked through Cal.com; the details you enter there are governed by Cal.com's privacy policy and used only to run the call.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe; VNMSFX never sees or stores your card number. Stripe's privacy policy governs the checkout.

Email

If you write to brandon@vnmsfx.com, the correspondence is kept as an ordinary business record and never sold or shared for marketing.

Client data

Data handled inside an engagement — your Shopify, QuickBooks, statements — is covered separately and more strictly: see the security page.

Your rights

Ask what's held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted: brandon@vnmsfx.com. Answered by a person, within a week.
