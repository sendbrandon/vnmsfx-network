BEFORE YOU START

PRODUCT DETAILS,
DEADLINES AND REVISIONS.

What do I need to provide?

Product information and descriptions, product and packaging assets, your intended audience, approved claims and your deadline. A short brief is enough for the first inquiry; we collect production assets after agreeing the project.

Can you meet my launch date?

Include the date in your brief and tell me if it’s fixed. I check the work involved, required assets and availability before we agree a delivery date.

What can I change during revisions?

Each included round is one collected set of feedback from your team, within the approved direction. A new idea or a changed brief is reviewed and priced separately.

Can we keep using the music?

Music created for your ad is licensed forever for the commercial and versions I deliver. No recurring music fee. You get usage rights, not ownership; new ads need a separate license.

Does this include running the ads?

This offer covers making the commercial and agreed assets. Ad placement, media spend and campaign management are separate.
