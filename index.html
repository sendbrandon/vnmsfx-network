Before the system, the work lived in inboxes and memory. The campaign could only grow as large as one person could chase by hand.

Frequently asked questions

How long does this take?

Thirty minutes for the free teardown. The Audit is delivered within five business days of the Audit Ready Date. Audit Ready means the agreement and data schedule are signed, payment has cleared, the agreed records, access and client decisions are ready, and the two-business-day readiness check has passed. The Build runs for three months. Its first Working Check is live within 10 business days of the Build Ready Date, all three are live by the end of week eight, and month three covers reliability, training and handover.

What if you disappear?

Everything lives in your accounts, in plain text, readable by any competent human. That's the point of it.

Why not just hire someone?

An ops hire fixes this by working the spreadsheets every night. This fixes it so nobody has to. Three months, and the knowledge lives in files you own — not in someone's head.

We don't use any AI tools yet

Approved software, AI, API, automation and hosting costs are client-owned and separate from the $15,000. Before Build Ready, the signed scope lists the exact products, existing versus new costs, expected monthly range, usage assumptions and spending limits.

We already use ChatGPT

Using ChatGPT is asking a smart stranger questions. This wires it into your orders, your vendors, your history — so the answers are about you.

We already bought a CRM once

So did most of my clients. That's the point — this isn't another tool to adopt. It's the tools you kept, finally connected.

What if the Audit finds nothing?

I will not invent a leak to justify a Build. Before the Audit starts, we agree in writing on the three questions, the records involved and what counts as a real issue. If the completed Audit tests all three questions and cannot document at least one qualifying operating issue under those written rules, I refund the full $2,500. You keep the completed report. If the records fail the readiness check before work starts, you choose a written rescope or a full refund. If you remove or materially change the required access after work starts and it is not restored within five business days, the Audit ends without a refund and you receive the completed work to date. Missing records never become a finding.

Is our data safe?

Your official records stay in your systems, and the working project lives in a restricted workspace your company owns. I use a named account, approved connection or export you create — never a shared password or personal AI account. Standard workflows read the agreed records. Findings and drafts are written only to the client-owned destination named in the agreement. If a provider grants broader technical permission, you see and approve that fact first — or you provide recurring exports instead. The standard service does not change books, orders, inventory, payments or send messages. If a temporary local file is necessary, it stays encrypted and outside cloud sync, then is deleted after processing and no later than seven days. Before access, the agreement names every source, permission, AI product and data-keeping rule. AI can help read and sort records. It is never the calculator or final judge. Important numbers must match the source records and pass a repeatable check outside AI. If they do not match, the answer is held for review instead of presented as fact. The full data flow is public: the security page.
